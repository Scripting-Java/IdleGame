## Visual Elements

Images will either be sourced from the internet or hand drawn with vectorpea. The overall theme will be cozy fantasy with soft lines, but this is subject to change based on how well I can draw it and whether something similar is available online. AI images may or may not be used based on generation quality and time, but I will try to prioritize other sources.

### 1. Business Artwork

Each business will have its own illustration or icon.

Initial business artwork:
- Farms: 
Wheat Farm - Wheat crop
Lettuce Farm - Lettuce
Milk Farm - Glass milk bottle
Horse Farm - Horse

- Workshops: 
Bakery - Cookie
Restaurant - Plate w/ fork
Watchmaker - Clock

- Industry: 
Lumber Mill - Tree 
Mine - Diamond

- Factories: 
Electronics Factory - Lightning Bolt
Car Factory - Sedan - Probably sourced online
Robotics Lab - Robot head emoji or something similar



### 2. Upgrade Artwork

Upgrade artwork will be entirely text based. The business logo will be displayed instead.

### 3. Overall Game Logos:

- Time Forge logo.
- Simplified browser icon.
- Currency icon.
- One icon for each business category: Farms, Workshops, Industry, and Factories.
- Maybe a start screen design if I get time.

### 3.1 Sizing Rules:

Logo images will be scaled to: **512 x 512**
TimeForge Logo: **1200 x 400**
Currency: **256 x 256** 

### 4. Main Interface

What needs to be shown:
- Current money balance - Top Right
- Total income per second - Top Right
- Businesses - Center of screen on background, each business will have it's own tiny building without overlap. They will not scale based on number and to upgrade them, you would click on this icon.
- Next Business(All info: purchase cost, base dollars/second, progress towards unlock, etc.) - Bottom menu of screen.
- Upgrade levels, costs, and effects - Individual per building. Click on each building/factory to find out. 
- Locked and unlocked businesses - Side Menu
- Navigation between categories and upgrades - Mouse. Arrow keys if I get time.

These visuals will be built based on simple reference images and font styles as opposed to full image drawings.

### 5. Background and Decoration

Background will be a simple day -> night fade with a simple animated sun. There might ocassionally be shooting stars or other tiny details if I get time.

In the future, I might add specific designs and decorations for each tier or so.

### 6. UI Icons and States

One icon set will cover common actions and indicators, including purchasing, upgrading, settings, money, locks, and milestones. These components will be CSS where they can be reused(e.g. fonts, text style, formatting spacing) and hand-drawn images for logos.

### 7. Future Artwork

Later, I might add achievements, prestige mechanics, additional worlds(space-related design tier), special milestone effects, and animated backgrounds(e.g. steam for the factory tier).
